THE ARTIST

ABOUT
DYCARTER

DyCarter is an Angolan singer, songwriter, producer, actor and writer creating across music, storytelling and visual art.

Based in Luanda, Angola, DyCarter's work moves between alternative R&B, alternative pop, literature, cinema and creative expression.

Music, stories, characters, images and ideas become different pieces of the same evolving universe.
